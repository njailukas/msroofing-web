FAQ

What people ask us most

Didn't find your answer? Call +420 774 157 194, Mon–Fri 7am–5pm, Sat 8am–12pm.

How much does a turnkey roof cost per m²?

The price depends on how complex the roof structure is and which covering you choose (tiles, asphalt shingles or Gerard and Satjam metal). Every roof is different, so we prepare an exact quote free of charge from your project documents or an on-site survey.

How long does a turnkey roof take?

For a typical family house (about 120–180 m² of roof), building the new timber frame, battening and laying the covering usually takes 7 to 21 working days, depending on complexity and weather.

What warranty do you give on the roof?

We give a 10-year warranty on carpentry, roof trusses and roofing work. Materials carry the manufacturer's warranty, up to 30–50 years for tiles and Gerard and Satjam systems.

Which countries do you work in?

We build roofs in the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Germany and the Netherlands. Thanks to our experience abroad, we meet the building standards of all these countries.

What do you need to prepare a quote?

Ideally the project documents. For renovations, a few photos of the roof, rough dimensions, the location and your preferred covering are enough. We reply within 24 hours.
