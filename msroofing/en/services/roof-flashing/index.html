About the service

Most leaks start at the metalwork

The covering itself rarely leaks. Problems start where the roof meets a chimney or wall, in a valley or at the eaves. That's why we do metalwork as a craft, with an eye for detail – as part of a new roof or on its own for an existing one.

Build-up

Metalwork on the roof

Every piece of metal on a roof has a job. Keep scrolling to see where each one goes.

  1. 1

    Roof covering

    Metalwork joins the covering and must form one watertight surface with it.

  2. 2

    Ridge

    Ridge flashing seals the highest point of the roof against rain and snow.

  3. 3

    Verge trim

    Protects the side edge of the roof from water and wind.

  4. 4

    Drip edge

    Leads water from the covering into the gutter, so it doesn't run down the boarding and facade.

  5. 5

    Gutter and brackets

    An eaves gutter hung on brackets with the right fall to the downpipe.

  6. 6

    Outlet and downpipe

    Carries water from the gutter safely to the ground, away from the foundations.

  7. 7

    Chimney flashing

    The most common leak point. We make the flashing around the chimney by hand so it fits exactly.

We also do metalwork on its own. A leak around the chimney or need new gutters? You don't have to replace the whole roof.

FAQ

Your questions

Didn't find your answer? Call +420 774 157 194, Mon–Fri 7am–5pm, Sat 8am–12pm.

It leaks around my chimney. Do I need a new roof?

Usually not. Often new chimney flashing and a repair of the adjoining covering is enough. During the inspection we find exactly where the water gets in.

Titanium zinc, copper or aluminium?

Titanium zinc is durable and develops a natural patina over time. Copper is very long-lasting and prestigious. Aluminium is light and more affordable. We'll advise based on your house and budget.

Do you replace just gutters and downpipes?

Yes, we supply and fit gutters, outlets and downpipes on their own for existing roofs.
