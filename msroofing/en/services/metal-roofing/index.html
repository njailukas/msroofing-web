About the service

A modern look without loading the frame

Gerard and Satjam metal roofing is much lighter than tiles, so on renovations the existing structure is often enough. Special surface coatings make it long-lasting and good-looking. We install it with standing-seam chimney flashing and every metalwork detail.

Build-up

Metal roof build-up

Metal is light, but it needs the right base and good ventilation. These are the six layers we build under and with it.

  1. 1

    Rafters

    The structure. With metal, even an older frame is often enough because the covering is so light.

  2. 2

    Breathable membrane

    Especially important under metal. It drains condensation and protects the structure from moisture.

  3. 3

    Counter battens

    A ventilated gap under the metal, letting moisture and heat escape.

  4. 4

    Battens

    Batten spacing matches the module of the chosen roofing, so every screw lands exactly where it should.

  5. 5

    Metal roofing

    Gerard or Satjam sheets fixed with sealed system screws.

  6. 6

    Flashing

    Ridge, verges, standing-seam chimney flashing and eaves. The details that decide whether a roof stays dry.

A metal roof needs good ventilation. That's why we never skip the membrane and counter battens – they prevent condensation under the metal.

FAQ

Your questions

Didn't find your answer? Call +420 774 157 194, Mon–Fri 7am–5pm, Sat 8am–12pm.

Isn't a metal roof noisy in the rain?

With the right build-up of membrane, counter battens and insulation, rain is barely audible in the attic. Stone-chip coated roofing dampens it even more.

Do I need to strengthen the frame for metal?

Usually not. Metal is very light, so on renovations the existing frame is normally enough. We always check its condition though.

What warranty does metal roofing have?

Gerard and Satjam materials carry a long-term manufacturer's warranty of up to 30–50 years depending on the type. We give 10 years on installation.
