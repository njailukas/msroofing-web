About the service

A long-lasting roof starts with the frame

Every pitched roof rests on a stable timber structure. We work with quality timber and precise joints, learned on demanding sites in Germany and the Netherlands. On renovations we first inspect the frame thoroughly and propose what to repair, strengthen or replace.

Build-up

How we build a roof structure

A roof frame is made of members that together carry the weight of the roof, snow and wind. Keep scrolling to see how we put it together.

  1. 1

    Wall plates

    Timbers anchored to the external walls. They transfer the load of the whole roof into the walls of the house.

  2. 2

    Ceiling joists

    Horizontal beams that form the attic floor and take the horizontal thrust from the rafters.

  3. 3

    Rafters

    The sloping members that carry the whole roof build-up and covering. Spacing and size are chosen for the covering and snow load.

  4. 4

    Ridge beam

    A long timber under the ridge that stiffens the rafters and keeps the whole frame in line.

  5. 5

    Collar ties

    Horizontal ties between opposite rafters. They stop them sagging and spreading.

We treat the timber against pests and mould before fitting it, so it's protected even where nobody can reach later.

FAQ

Your questions

Didn't find your answer? Call +420 774 157 194, Mon–Fri 7am–5pm, Sat 8am–12pm.

Will my old roof structure carry a new covering?

It depends on the condition of the timber and its sizes. We inspect the frame on site. If needed, we strengthen it or recommend lightweight metal roofing.

How can I tell if the frame is infested?

Typical signs are insect exit holes, crumbling wood, mould or sagging rafters. If you're unsure, we'll take a look.

Do you build pergolas and canopies too?

Yes, we build timber structures for pergolas, carports and canopies.
