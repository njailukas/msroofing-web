About the service

A flat roof lives or dies by its details

Flat roofs usually leak at parapets, penetrations and outlets, not in the middle. That's why every renovation starts with thorough substrate preparation, and every seam, junction and corner is carefully done by hand.

Build-up

Flat roof build-up

A flat roof has to withstand standing water, frost and sun. These are the six layers that protect it, from the structure up.

  1. 1

    Structural deck

    A concrete slab or timber deck. It must be level, clean and dry – the substrate decides the quality of everything above it.

  2. 2

    Vapour barrier

    Stops moisture from inside the building getting into the insulation and reducing its performance.

  3. 3

    Thermal insulation

    Boards laid staggered and without gaps, with tapered insulation where needed so water flows to the outlets.

  4. 4

    Base membrane

    The first waterproofing layer, forming a reliable base for the cap sheet.

  5. 5

    Cap sheet with granules

    IPA modified bitumen membrane, torch-applied. Slate granules protect it from UV.

  6. 6

    Parapet and flashing

    Waterproofing turned up the parapet and capped with flashing. The riskiest spot on a flat roof, so we take the most care here.

Two layers of bitumen membrane mean double protection. Even if the cap sheet gets damaged, water won't reach the structure straight away.

FAQ

Your questions

Didn't find your answer? Call +420 774 157 194, Mon–Fri 7am–5pm, Sat 8am–12pm.

Repair a flat roof or replace it?

It depends on the state of the substrate and insulation. If the insulation is dry, new waterproofing is often enough. If it's wet, a complete new build-up pays off. We'll assess it during a free inspection.

Why two layers of bitumen membrane?

A two-layer system is tougher and more reliable. The base membrane forms the first waterproofing layer, and the granulated cap sheet protects it from UV and mechanical damage.

Do you do small flat roofs, like garages?

Yes. We do flat roofs on garages, extensions, family houses and apartment blocks.
