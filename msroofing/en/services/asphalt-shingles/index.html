About the service

Shingles handle shapes where others give up

Asphalt shingles are light, flexible and dampen the sound of rain well. That makes them ideal for roofs with complex shapes, dormers or steep pitches. The key to a long life is solid full boarding and properly finished details.

Build-up

Shingle roof build-up

Shingles need a solid, level base. These are the six layers we build from the rafters to the ridge.

  1. 1

    Rafters

    The structure we fix the full boarding to.

  2. 2

    Full boarding

    A solid, level base of boards or panels. Without it, shingles hold neither their shape nor a seal.

  3. 3

    Underlayment

    A waterproofing layer under the shingles. Insurance in case wind lifts a shingle.

  4. 4

    Drip edge and verge trim

    Metal edging that leads water away from the boarding.

  5. 5

    Shingles

    Laid in bond and nailed to the manufacturer's spec. The self-sealing strip bonds them into one surface in the warmth.

  6. 6

    Ridge cap and ventilation

    Ridge of shingle caps with ventilation, so no moisture builds up under the boarding.

A shingle is only as good as the boarding under it. That's why we always check it on renovations and replace damaged boards.

FAQ

Your questions

What roof pitch suits shingles?

Shingles suit mainly steep and complex roofs. The minimum pitch is set by the shingle manufacturer – we'll tell you during the survey whether they suit your roof.

Why is full boarding needed?

Shingles are flexible and don't carry themselves. Boarding gives them a solid, level base to nail into. Without it, the roof wouldn't be watertight.

How long do shingles last?

Their life depends on the quality of the shingle and the installation. Quality shingles carry a long manufacturer's warranty. We give 10 years on our work.
